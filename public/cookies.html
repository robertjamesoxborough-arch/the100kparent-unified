Cookie & Local Storage Notice

Last updated: July 2026

The short version

We don't use advertising, analytics or cross-site tracking cookies, and we don't build a profile of you. The only data the site keeps on your device is what's needed to run the savings calculator and show your report — and that stays in your browser.

What we store on your device

  • Calculator inputs and results (local storage). When you use the calculator, the figures you enter — your income, any partner's income, the number and ages of your children, monthly childcare cost, employment type and pension contributions — and the results are saved in your browser's local storage (for example under the key 100kp_results). This lets us generate your on-screen report and PDF on your own device. It is not transmitted to or stored on our servers.
  • Strictly necessary cookies. Our hosting provider (Vercel) may set a small number of essential cookies to run and secure the site — for example, load balancing or protecting against abuse. These do not track you across other websites.

What we do not use

No analytics cookies, no advertising or retargeting cookies, and no social-media tracking pixels. We do not sell or share your data with advertisers. Web fonts are self-hosted on our own domain, so simply viewing the site does not share your IP address with a third-party font provider.

Clearing this data

Your calculator inputs and results stay in your browser until you remove them. You can clear them at any time through your browser's "clear site data" / local storage controls for this site, or by using a private / incognito window. Clearing the data won't break anything — you'll just need to re-enter your figures next time.

Do you need to consent?

Because we only use strictly necessary storage and essential cookies — and nothing for tracking or advertising — UK law (PECR and UK GDPR) does not require a consent banner for them. If we ever introduce non-essential cookies, we will ask for your consent first and update this notice.

More information

This notice sits alongside our Privacy Policy, which explains how we handle personal data more generally. Questions about cookies or your data: privacy@the100kparent.co.uk.
