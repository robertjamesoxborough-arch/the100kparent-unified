2. Information We Collect

  • Calculator inputs (your income, any partner's income, number and ages of children, monthly childcare cost, employment type and pension contributions): these are stored only in your own browser (using local storage) so we can generate your on-screen report. 3. How and Why We Use It (Lawful Bases)

  • To deliver the report you buy and provide support — lawful basis: performance of a contract.
  • To send purchase confirmations and product emails — lawful basis: contract (transactional) and consent (marketing; you can opt out at any time).
  • To operate, secure and improve the site — lawful basis: our legitimate interests.
  • To refer you to an independent financial adviser — only where you ask us to; lawful basis: consent.

4. Who We Share It With (Processors)

We use a small number of trusted providers who act as our data processors under written data-processing agreements:

  • Stripe — payment processing.
  • Loops — sending purchase confirmation and product emails.
  • Vercel — website hosting.

Some of these providers are based outside the UK/EEA. Where personal data is transferred internationally, it is protected by appropriate safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Standard Contractual Clauses. We do not sell your personal data.

5. How Long We Keep It

Calculator inputs remain in your browser until you clear them. Purchase and transaction records are kept for as long as needed to provide the service and to meet legal and tax obligations (generally up to 6 years). Marketing contact details are kept until you unsubscribe.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

We use your browser's local storage to hold your calculator inputs so your report can be generated on your device. This is essential to the tool and stays on your device. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Web fonts are self-hosted on our own domain, so viewing the site does not share your data with a third-party font provider. See our Cookie & Local Storage Notice for full details.
