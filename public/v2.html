Quick answers

How is the saving calculated?

We combine four strategies: Tax-Free Childcare (20% top-up, max £2k/child), salary sacrifice (income tax + NI savings), income splitting between partners, and the value of 30 hours free childcare if eligible. Your result is personalised to your income and family situation.

What if I don’t save money?

Full refund. 30 days. No questions.

Can I speak to someone?

Yes — the Complete Guide (£49) includes a free 30-minute call with our childcare savings advisor.

Is this just about going under £100k?

No. We cover all income levels and a wide range of strategies — not just income reduction.
