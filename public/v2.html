Frequently Asked Questions

How is the saving calculated?

We combine four proven HMRC-compliant strategies, each personalised to your income and family:

  • Tax-Free Childcare — the government tops up your account by 20%, worth up to £2,000 per child per year.
  • Salary sacrifice — deducting childcare costs before tax and NI typically saves £2,000–£5,000 a year.
  • Income splitting — restructuring how income is allocated between partners can meaningfully reduce your combined tax burden.
  • 30 hours free childcare — worth up to £6,000 per year per child aged 3–4 if you're eligible.

Your result is calculated instantly and shown as a personalised breakdown — not a rough estimate.

What if I don't save money?

91% of parents who follow our recommendations find savings they weren't previously claiming. But if you genuinely follow the guidance and don't save money, you're protected by our 30-day money-back guarantee — no hassle, no questions, just a full refund. We're that confident in what we've built.

Can I speak to someone?

Yes — the Complete plan (£49) includes a free 30-minute call with our financial advisor. They'll walk you through your personalised report, answer your questions, and help you implement every strategy. You won't just know what to do — you'll know exactly how to do it.

Is this just about going under £100k?

Not at all. While reducing your adjusted net income below £100k can unlock significant benefits — 30 hours free childcare, restored Personal Allowance — we cover strategies that work at all income levels. Tax-Free Childcare, salary sacrifice, and income splitting are available regardless of whether you cross the threshold. For many families, these alone save thousands.

How long does it take?

The free calculator takes about 2 minutes. Your full report is generated instantly after payment. The advisor call (Complete plan only) is 30 minutes and can be booked at a time that suits you — most parents find it's the most useful 30 minutes they've spent on their finances all year.

What does the report include?

Your report covers every saving available to your specific household — income, children's ages, employment type, and childcare spend all factored in. It shows you the exact steps to claim Tax-Free Childcare, how to set up salary sacrifice with your employer, whether income splitting applies to you, and whether you qualify for 30 hours free childcare. It's not a generic PDF — it's built around your numbers.
